## Contract implication

The prior editorial schema treated independently passing finalists as effectively equivalent and selected the first all-passing option. The comparative editorial contract introduced after this review requires unique ranks, one eligible rank-1 recommendation, material strengths and weaknesses, direct winner comparisons, and auditable spoken-rhyme evidence. Human selections above remain the authoritative findings for this completed run.
